Built for real field work

Designed around how carbon inventories are actually done in miombo and other African woodlands.

Offline

Installs on the phone and works with no signal. All data is kept on the device until you share or back it up.

±4 m GPS

Plot centres are only accepted at the accuracy you set (0.1–4 m). Several fixes are averaged, and the achieved accuracy is recorded.

8 models

Peer-reviewed African allometric models: Chidumayo (2012), Chave (2005, 2014), Mugasha (2013), Kachamba (2016) and Ryan (2011).

2,561 names

Tree names in Bemba, Nyanja, Tonga, Lozi, Kaonde, Lunda, Kunda, Senga, Tumbuka and English, with wood density from the Global Wood Density Database.

95% CI

Averages by stratum and site with confidence intervals, plus a stratified project estimate with a 90% confidence interval.

Excel

An Excel workbook in which every result is a live formula, plus CSV, KML and GPX (WGS84) and backup to Google Drive or OneDrive.
